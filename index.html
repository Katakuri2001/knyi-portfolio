01Background

Freelance content writing and Facebook marketing experience from 2023–2026, with a developing specialization in performance marketing.

How I think

A campaign built on a wrong diagnosis cannot be rescued by better creative. So the diagnosis comes first, every time.

Research-led approach. I always start by understanding the business problem, the audience, and the competitive landscape before developing strategy.

  1. 01What is the business problem?
  2. 02Who is the audience, and who is not?
  3. 03What is already being said in this market?

What I value

Strategic thinking, creative quality, and data-driven optimization. Results that serve the business objective.

Experience to date

Freelance content writing and Facebook marketing, 2023–2026, alongside graphic design, content writing and digital marketing training. Currently studying Digital Marketing Advanced.

Figure — research and planning notes

Audience and competitor research is how a plan starts. Segments are mapped before spend is allocated, and the map is revised once real campaign data comes back.

That loop — research, plan, read the data, revise the read — is the part of the job that decides whether creative is worth producing in the first place.

04Working method

Four stages,
in order, every time.

Research feeds the plan, execution feeds the measurement, and the measurement feeds the next round of research. Skipping the first stage is the most expensive mistake available.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Establish what is actually wrong before spending anything on it.

    • Business problem analysis
    • Market & competitor research
    • Objective clarification
    • Offer assessment
  2. 02

    Strategize

    Decide who the campaign is for and what it has to change.

    • Audience & competitor research
    • Positioning & messaging
    • Content pillars development
    • Campaign structuring
  3. 03

    Plan

    Turn the strategy into media, creative and budget decisions.

    • Creative direction
    • Media planning & channel mix
    • Budget allocation
    • Measurement framework
  4. 04↺ loops back

    Optimize

    Read performance, find the opportunity, iterate on it.

    • Performance monitoring
    • Opportunity identification
    • Data-driven iteration
    • Continuous improvement

Inputs

Business objectives, audience research, competitor research, offer analysis.

Decisions

Objectives, audience, positioning, creative direction, channel mix, budget.

Feedback

Performance monitoring, opportunity identification, data-driven iteration.
